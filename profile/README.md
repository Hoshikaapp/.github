## What makes Hoshika different

| | |
|---|---|
| 📊 **Your list, one tap** | Track anime & manga with one-tap episode progress, scores and custom lists |
| 🎬 **Watch parties** | Synced playback, live chat and screen share — watch together, wherever you are |
| ⚔️ **Challenges & clans** | Send anime to friends, climb clan leaderboards, win clan wars |
| 🧭 **Real discovery** | Seasonal charts, trending now, and recommendations that learn your taste |
| ⭐ **Canon Score** | Community ratings *and* verified-critic scores, side by side |
| 🎁 **Wrapped** | Your year in anime, beautifully summarized |
| 🔁 **2-click import** | Bring your list from MyAnimeList or AniList — and keep it in sync |
